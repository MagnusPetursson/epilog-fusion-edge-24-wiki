02The machine

What moves, and why

A beam you can’t see, four mirrors, one lens and a table that rises to meet it. Turn the model; pick one of the nine faults in its side panel to watch it happen.

What the model is and isn’t. The model shows the 1055 × 834 × 967 mm machine envelope and the 610 × 610 mm work area.[1][17] The inside is schematic. Do not use this model to locate hidden components for service. The CO₂ beam is drawn red so you can see it; the real one is invisible.

How the mechanisms work
cabinet
A closed sheet-metal cabinet on a built-in pedestal with four wheels. It fully encloses the laser and its beam path; two interlocks switch the beam off when the lid opens. The access panels are not interlocked, so they stay on.[1,2]
source
An air-cooled CO₂ tube in the rear bay. Saga’s unit is 80 W, Model 17000, manufactured September 2021. The April 2021 Fusion Edge 12/24 manual covers this generation. Its light is 10.6 µm: infrared, invisible, and it doesn’t pass through the window.[4]
beam path
Four mirrors and one window optic carry the beam forward, across the X-beam and down into the head. The last mirror turns it down through the lens. Dirty optics lose power and get damaged.[2,11]
head
The lens focuses the beam to a small spot a fixed distance below the cone. The standard focal length is 50.8 mm; the installed lens has not yet been verified. Ask lab staff before choosing a lens-dependent setup. Cone air assist blows through the head whenever a job runs, keeping the lens clean and flames down.[1,2]
motion
Brushless DC servo motors with encoders move the X-beam (Y) and the head along it (X), on Kevlar belts. The table moves in Z: it rises until your material’s surface sits at focus.[2,4]
red dot
A visible red laser runs in line with the invisible CO₂ beam. It shows where the head is, and Trace uses it to outline your job.[2]
table
The vector grid holds your sheet above the tray so the beam passes through without reflecting back. Cut-outs fall into the tray, where they become fuel.[2,5]
extraction
The fan pulls air from front to back, through the rear plenum and out of one 102 mm port. Epilog asks for 595–680 m³/h of extraction. At Saga it runs all the time.[2]
control panel
On the right shoulder: a 178 mm touch display, a joystick and Go/Stop. The ring around Go/Stop shows status; red means a drive has failed. The left shoulder carries the fire-safety label, not a button.[2,10]
The machine in numbers
outside
1055 × 834 × 967 mm, 102 kg, on a built-in pedestal with wheels.[1]
work area
610 × 610 mm; material up to 254 mm thick.[1]
table
27.2 kg while it moves (autofocus moves it), 40.8 kg resting. These are Saga’s table limits. The 2025 family manuals list lower limits: 11.4 kg moving and 18.1 kg resting. Do not transfer limits between machines.[1,13]
laser
80 W CO₂, 10.6 µm, air-cooled metal/ceramic tube.[4]
lens
Standard focal length: 50.8 mm. The installed lens has not yet been verified; ask lab staff.[1]
extraction
595–680 m³/h, one 102 mm port at the back.[2]
air
8 mm receptacle at the back, 2.1 bar maximum.[2]
power
Entry at the right side, rear corner.[2]
The status ring on Go/Stop

Red means a drive has failed: leave the machine and call staff.[2,10]
